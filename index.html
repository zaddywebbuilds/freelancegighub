Before you ask

Fair questions.

Is this just scraped job boards?

No. These are posts real people wrote on LinkedIn and X, usually the founder or the hiring manager themselves. That's why so many of them have a WhatsApp number or an email address right there in the post.

How many alerts will I get?

It depends on your field and how tight your terms are. Some days a handful, some days more. It is deliberately built to send you fewer, better alerts rather than flooding you. Anything the filter isn't confident about never gets sent.

Do I still have to write the message myself?

Yes, and you should. GigScout gives you the opening angle so you know what to lead with, but the words are yours. That's exactly why it works. You're a person replying, not a template.

What if I'm not on Upwork or Fiverr at all?

Even better. This is a way to get client work without ever making an account on either of them.

What if I don't know what search terms to use?

You don't need to. You describe your service in your own words and it writes the terms for you, then shows them to you before using them. You can change them any time, or ask for a fresh set.

How do I cancel?

Then do nothing. There is no subscription to cancel, because nothing recurs. Your 30 days run out, the alerts stop, and you are never charged again unless you decide to pay for another 30.
